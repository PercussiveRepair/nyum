## Chicken wings

---

* `900g` chicken wings
* `1 tbsp` peanut oil, or vegetable oil
* `1 tbsp` Shaoxing wine, or dry sherry
* `1 tbsp` light soy sauce, or soy sauce
* `1 tsp` ground ginger
* `1 tsp` sugar
* `1¼ tsp` salt
* `½ tsp` garlic powder
* `¼ tsp` white pepper powder
* `2 tbsp` cornstarch
* `2 tbsp` all-purpose flour
* Oil spray

> Add the chicken wings, peanut oil, Shaoxing wine, soy sauce, ground ginger, sugar, salt, garlic powder, and white pepper powder to a large bowl or large ziplock bag. Mix well and massage the chicken (or the bag) until the seasonings are evenly coated. Marinate in the fridge overnight (highly recommended), or for a minimum of 2 hours at room temperature.
>
> Right before cooking, preheat the air fryer to 176°C.
>
> Drain the chicken thoroughly, discard the marinating liquid, and pat the chicken dry with paper towels. Stir the cornstarch and flour together in a small bowl, add to the chicken, and mix to coat well. It should form a dry-ish coating on the chicken with some dry flour on the surface.
>
> Place the wings in the air fryer basket in a single layer, skin side down. Cook in batches if they don't fit. Spray with oil so there's no dry flour left.
>
> Air fry for 12 to 15 minutes, flipping once in between, until golden and crispy.

---

> To make this gluten free, use dry sherry in place of the Shaoxing wine.
>
> Flipping the chicken once during cooking crisps the wings more evenly, but the result is also really good without flipping. If you do not plan to flip, place the wings skin side up. For smaller wings, flip at the 6-minute mark. For larger wings, flip at the 8-minute mark.
