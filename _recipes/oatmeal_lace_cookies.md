## Lace cookies

---

* `115g` unsalted butter
* `140g` brown sugar
* `100g` rolled oats
* `1 tbsp` maple syrup
* `1 tsp` vanilla extract
* `½ tsp` salt

> Preheat the oven to 190°C (175°C convection). Line 2 baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone baking mats. Set aside.
>
> In a medium saucepan, melt the butter and sugar on medium-low heat, stirring frequently until the mixture has a liquid consistency. Turn off the heat.
>
> Stir in the oats, maple syrup, vanilla and salt. The mixture will be thick and glossy, and will continue to thicken the longer it sits.
>
> Place 1 tsp of the oat mixture onto the baking sheet, leaving about 5cm between cookies to allow for their spread. Bake for 8-9 minutes, until golden brown on the edges and flat in appearance.
>
> Allow the cookies to cool completely on the baking sheet, then remove carefully.
>
> Optional: sandwich the cookies with 1 tsp of melted chocolate or chocolate-hazelnut spread spread over one of the cookie sides.

---

> These cookies are very delicate, so handle gently. They continue to harden as they cool.
>
> After cooling, store at room temperature in an airtight container for up to 1 week.
>
> Steel cut oats can be substituted 1:1 for rolled oats. Maple syrup can be substituted with honey or corn syrup.
