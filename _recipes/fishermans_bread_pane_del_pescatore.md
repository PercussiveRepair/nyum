## Fisherman's bread

---

* `½ cup` raisins
* `300g` all-purpose flour
* `½ cup` granulated sugar
* `½ tsp` baking powder
* `pinch` salt
* `⅓ cup` butter, softened
* `2 large` eggs, room temperature
* `2 large` egg yolks, room temperature
* `3 tbsp` pine nuts
* `¼ cup` almonds, coarsely chopped
* `3-4 tbsp` sliced almonds

> Preheat the oven to 180°C. Line 1-2 baking sheets with parchment paper.
>
> Soak the raisins in boiling water or rum for 20 minutes, then drain well. Set aside.
>
> In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, salt and baking powder. Add the softened butter, eggs and yolks and mix until almost combined. Add the pine nuts, raisins and chopped almonds and mix to combine. The dough should be soft.
>
> Move the dough to a lightly floured flat surface and form into 10-12 balls. Place them on the prepared baking sheet, flatten very slightly, brush with the sugar water, and sprinkle with the sliced almonds. Bake for approximately 20-25 minutes. Let cool for 10 minutes on the baking sheet, then move to a wire rack to cool completely.

## Sugar water

---

* `1-2 tsp` granulated sugar
* `2-3 tbsp` water

> In a small pot, heat the sugar and water until boiling, then continue to cook for 2-3 minutes. Let cool to room temperature before brushing. Alternatively, brush with a slightly beaten egg white or even milk.

---

> Store in an airtight container in a cool dry place for up to 3 days. Some people find these better as they get drier in storage.
>
> To freeze: wrap the completely cooled buns in foil, then place in a freezer-safe container or bag. They keep for up to a month. Thaw the frozen buns on the counter.
