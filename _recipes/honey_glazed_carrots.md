## Honey glazed carrots

---

* `900g` medium carrots (about 8), peeled and cut crosswise into 2-inch pieces (if more than 1-inch thick, halve or quarter lengthwise into pieces roughly 1/2 to 3/4-inch thick; otherwise leave whole)
* `45g` unsalted butter
* `3 cloves` garlic, minced
* `1 1/2 tbsp` honey
* `1 tsp` kosher salt
* `1/4 tsp` freshly ground black pepper
* `1/2` small lemon, juiced
* `` chopped fresh parsley leaves, for garnish (optional)

> Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat the oven to 425F (220C). Place the carrot pieces on a rimmed baking sheet.
>
> Melt the butter in a small saucepan over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook, swirling occasionally, until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Remove from the heat, add the honey, and whisk to combine.
>
> Drizzle half the honey glaze over the carrots and toss to coat. Season with the salt and pepper and toss again, then spread into an even layer.
>
> Roast for 15 minutes. Toss the carrots, then roast until tender and browned on the bottoms, 15 to 20 minutes more.
>
> Drizzle over the remaining honey glaze, squeeze the lemon over the carrots, and toss to coat. Transfer to a serving bowl and garnish with parsley, if using.

---

> Make ahead: the carrots can be peeled and chopped up to 1 day ahead. Refrigerate in an airtight container.
>
> Storage: leftovers can be refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 5 days.
