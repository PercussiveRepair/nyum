## Orzo salad

---

* `1½ cups` uncooked orzo pasta
* `2 (170g) cans` marinated artichoke hearts
* `1` cucumber, seeded and chopped
* `1` ripe tomato, seeded and chopped
* `1` red onion, chopped
* `1 cup` crumbled feta cheese
* `1 (55g) can` black olives, drained
* `¼ cup` chopped fresh parsley
* `1 tbsp` lemon juice
* `½ tsp` dried oregano
* `½ tsp` lemon pepper

> Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Add the pasta and cook until al dente, 8 to 10 minutes; drain.
>
> Drain the artichoke hearts, reserving the liquid.
>
> Combine the pasta, artichoke hearts, cucumber, tomato, onion, feta, olives, parsley, lemon juice, oregano, and lemon pepper in a large bowl and toss well. Chill in the refrigerator for 1 hour.
>
> Just before serving, drizzle the reserved artichoke marinade over the salad.

---

> Home cooks agree that using bottled Greek or Italian dressing gives an even better texture.
