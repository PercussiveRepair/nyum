* `200g` plain flour
* `2` eggs
* `½ tsp` salt
* `1 tbsp` olive oil

For lasagne, double the amounts above.

> Add all ingredients to stand mixer
>
> Mix for 5-10 mins until combined, and a smooth elastic dough forms
>
> wrap in cling film and place in the fridge for 30 mins
>
> use however.
