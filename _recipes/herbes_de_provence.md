title: Herbes de Provence 
category: Sauces & Seasonings
veggie: y

--- 
* `1 tbsp` fennel seeds
* `2 tbsp` dried rosemary
* `1/4 cup` dried thyme
* `3 tbsp` dried marjoram
* `3 tbsp` dried summer savory, optional
* `1 tbsp` dried tarragon
* `1 tsp` dried chervil, optional
* `1 tsp` dried mint
* `1 tsp` dried lavender, optional
 
> Grind together fennel and rosemary in pestle & mortar until they are a uniform size. Mix in the rest of the herbs and store in an airtight container
