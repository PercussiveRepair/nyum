## Sourdough discard waffles

---

* `200g` sourdough starter (discard, or fed and bubbly)
* `180g` whole milk (buttermilk can be substituted)
* `2` eggs
* `50g` butter, melted
* `150g` all-purpose flour
* `5g` salt (adjust to taste)
* `50g` sugar
* `10g` baking powder

> In a large bowl, mix the starter, milk, and eggs until well combined. Stir in the melted butter.
>
> In a separate bowl, combine the flour, salt, sugar, and baking powder.
>
> Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix until the batter is pourable.
>
> Heat the waffle iron, spoon in the batter, and cook until it reaches your preferred doneness. The waffles can be made soft or crispy. Don't overfill the waffle iron.
