* `2 cups` apple, peeled & diced small
* `1 tbsp` brown sugar
* `1/2 tsp` cinnamon
* `1 tbsp` lemon juice
* `1 pack` chilled, store bought puff pastry
* `1` egg, beaten
* Demarara sugar to sprinkle

> Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C). Line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
>
> In a large bowl, stir together the dicedapples, brown sugar, cinnamon and lemon juice.
>
> Unfold the puff pastry sheets onto a lightly floured work surface. Using a rolling pin, gently roll over the pastry to seal any perforations then cut each sheet into 4-6 squares about 10-15cm on a side depending on pastry size.
> 
> Using a slotted spoon, scoop a portion of the apple mixture into the center of each puff pastry square. 
>
> Run a wet finger along the edges then fold the pastry across diagonally to form triangles. Using a fork, crimp together the edges then transfer the turnovers to the prepared baking sheets, spacing them a few cm apart.
>
> Cut three small slits in the tops of each turnover then brush them with the egg. Sprinkle the turnovers with the demarara sugar. 
>
> At this point you can optionally freeze the turnovers on the trays and then store in sealed plastic bags in the freezer for up to 3 months.
> 
> Bake the turnovers for 17 to 20 minutes or until they’re golden brown and puffed. Transfer the turnovers to a rack to cool then serve warm with cream or custard.
