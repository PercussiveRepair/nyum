## Shrimp orzo

---

* `2 tsp` olive oil
* `5 cloves` garlic, minced
* `½ tsp` Italian seasoning
* `1⅓ cups` orzo pasta
* `700ml` chicken bone broth
* `¼ tsp` salt, or to taste
* `¼ tsp` freshly ground black pepper
* `225g` raw tiger shrimp, peeled and deveined, or more to taste
* `handful` spinach, cut into thin strips
* `1` lemon, zested
* Lemon juice, to taste
* `1 tbsp` grated Parmesan cheese

> Heat the olive oil in a deep skillet over medium heat. Add the garlic and Italian seasoning and sauté just until fragrant, 30 to 60 seconds.
>
> Add the orzo and stir to toast lightly, about 30 seconds. Pour in the broth, add the salt and pepper, and bring to a gentle boil.
>
> Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer, stirring occasionally, until the orzo is nearly tender and most of the liquid is absorbed, 7 to 9 minutes.
>
> Nestle the shrimp into the orzo. Scatter the spinach over the top. Add the lemon zest and lemon juice. Cover and cook the shrimp just until pink and opaque, turning once, 2 to 3 minutes per side.
>
> Turn off the heat. Stir in the Parmesan cheese. Adjust the seasoning to taste.
