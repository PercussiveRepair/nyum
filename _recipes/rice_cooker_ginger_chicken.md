title: Rice Cooker Ginger & Garlic Chicken
category: Asian
description: Aromatic, warming one pot meal
image: 

--- 
* `2` boneless chicken thighs
* `2 tbsp` light soy sauce
* `1 tsp` dark soy sauce
* `1 tbsp` shaoxing wine
* `1 tbsp` oyster sauce
* `1 tsp` sugar
* `1 tsp` sesame oil
* `1/4 tsp` white pepper
* `1 tbsp` cornflour
* `2 cloves` garlic, finely chopped
* `2 slices ` ginger
* `1.5 cups` jasmine or basmati rice
* `1` spring onion

> Cut chicken into cubes. Marinate the chicken with soy sauces, Shaoxing wine, oyster sauce, sugar, sesame oil, white pepper, cornflour, garlic, and ginger. Let it sit for 15 minutes.
>
> Wash rice and place in rice cooker. Reduce water slightly for the chicken juices.
>
> Layer chicken on top of the rice.
>
> Pour remaining marinade over the rice and chicken.
>
> Start the rice cooker and let it cook as usual.
>
> Once done, let it sit on "keep warm" mode for 10 minutes to allow the flavours to meld.
>
> Fluff the rice, garnish with spring onions, and serve.
