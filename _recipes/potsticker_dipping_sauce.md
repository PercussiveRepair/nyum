---
title: Potsticker Dipping Sauce 
category: Sauces & Seasonings
description: Quick Chinese dipping sauce of soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil and ginger
veggie: y

--- 
* `1 tbsp` light soy sauce
* `1 tbsp` rice vinegar
* `¼ tsp` sugar
* `Few drops` toasted sesame oil
* `Few shreds` fresh ginger

> Mix and serve
