## For the stew

---

* `3-4 tbsp` olive oil
* `700g` pork neck
* Salt
* Pepper
* `1` red bell pepper
* `1` green bell pepper
* `1` orange bell pepper
* `1` yellow bell pepper
* `1` onion
* `1 clove` garlic
* `1 pinch` chili flakes
* `1 tsp` cumin
* `1` chicken bouillon cube
* `400g` red wine
* `1 tbsp` tomato paste
* `1 tbsp` honey
* `200g` water

> Place a pot over high heat and add the olive oil.
>
> Cut the pork into 2-3cm pieces and add to the hot pot. Season with salt and pepper and sauté for 3-4 minutes until golden brown.
>
> Cut the peppers into thin strips and add them to the pot.
>
> Cut the onion and garlic into thin slices and add them to the pot.
>
> Add the chili flakes, cumin, and chicken bouillon cube, and deglaze the pot with the wine. Add the tomato paste, honey, and water, and stir.
>
> Cover with the lid and simmer over medium heat for 50-60 minutes.

## To serve

---

* Oregano
* Olive oil
* Pepper
* Feta cheese
* Bread

> Serve the stew with oregano, olive oil, pepper, feta cheese, and bread.
